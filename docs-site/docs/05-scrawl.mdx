[Scrawl](https://scrawl.kitlangton.com), by [Kit Langton](https://github.com/kitlangton/scrawl), records your screen while you talk, click and draw. jalco-drop turns a capture into a page anyone can open: the recording on the left, and the transcript with every marked moment on the right. Clicking a moment jumps the video there.

```bash
jalco-drop ~/Library/Application\ Support/scrawl/captures/scrawl-<id>.scrawl
```

The CLI spots a capture and uploads only what the page needs:

- the video, with your narration merged in (Scrawl records them separately, so this needs `ffmpeg`);
- the marked frames and the contact sheet;
- a trimmed `scrawl.json`, without the cursor track or the list of every window title on your screen.

Captures expire after 30 days unless you pass `-e`. Link previews carry `og:video`, so the recording can play inline where a chat app supports it.

:::warning
A capture shows whatever was on your screen, including browser tabs and bookmarks. Watch it before you share it.
:::
